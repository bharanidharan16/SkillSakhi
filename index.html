OUR SDG 8 COMMITMENT

Every student deserves an opportunity to grow.

SkillSakhi supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth) by helping students discover internship opportunities and develop career-ready skills. We encourage skill development, accessible career exploration, and awareness of employment opportunities through internship listings, smart search and filters, and learning resources.

✦ UN SDG 8: DECENT WORK AND ECONOMIC GROWTH ✦ Internship Discovery ✦ Career-Ready Skill Development ✦ Employment Opportunity Awareness

SkillSakhi is an academic prototype created for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH). Organizations, vacancies, and stipend figures shown are fictional demo entries designed to illustrate internship discovery, skill development, and alignment with UN Sustainable Development Goal 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth).
